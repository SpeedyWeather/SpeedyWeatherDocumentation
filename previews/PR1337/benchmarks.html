Benchmarks ​

Performance benchmarks for SpeedyWeather.jl across architectures. This page is auto-generated at doc-build time from SpeedyWeather/benchmark/assets/benchmark_results.json, which itself is updated by SpeedyWeather/benchmark/manual_benchmarking.jl. Running the benchmark script on a new architecture adds a column to the overview table, a series to each comparison figure, and a per-architecture section to this page.

All simulations are benchmarked over several seconds (wallclock time) without output. Benchmarking excludes initialization. By default each configuration is timed up to 5 times (as long as all its timed runs together take at most 30 s), each architecture's section states its settings. SYPD is the median and Spread shows how much the runs differ. Timings still vary between processes and machines, so treat the numbers as rough rather than precise.
