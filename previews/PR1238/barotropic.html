Barotropic vorticity model ​

The dynamical core presented here to solve the barotropic vorticity equations largely follows the idealized models with spectral dynamics developed at the Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory (NOAA Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory, 2013): a barotropic vorticity model (NOAA Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory, 2013).

Many concepts of the Shallow water model and the Primitive equation model are similar, such that for example horizontal diffusion and the Time integration are only explained here.

So scaling with the radius squared means we can use dimensionless operators, however, this comes at the cost of some constants like the diffusion coefficient needing to be scaled too during initialization, which may be confusing because values are not what users expect them to be. In general we try to scale as much as possible on the fly to hide it from the user. SpeedyWeather.jl follows the logic that the scaling to the prognostic variables is only applied just before the time integration and variables are unscaled for output and after the time integration finished. The scaled time step (seconds per meter) is applied on the fly within the time integration and is not exposed to the user. In hopefully many other cases it is clearly denoted that a variable or constant is scaled.
